Privacy policy

rclone Mac backup is a personal tool with one user, its owner. This policy covers the Google OAuth client of that name.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Google grants it the drive.file permission on the owner's Google Drive. That permission covers only the files this client creates: the backup copies it uploads from the owner's Mac. It cannot see or change any other file in the Drive, and it requests no other Google data.

What it does with that access

It copies files from the owner's Mac to the owner's Google Drive, and replaces or removes those copies as the originals change. The access token stays on the owner's Mac.

What it does not do

It does not collect, sell or share data with anyone, and no other person's data passes through it. It runs no analytics and has no server of its own.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's security settings, under third-party connections.
