rclone Mac backup

A personal, single-user Google OAuth client. The owner uses it with rclone to copy folders from his own Mac into his own Google Drive, one way, once an hour.

It is not a public service or a product. Nobody else signs in to it, and it offers nothing to sign up for.

It asks Google only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and change only the files it has created itself, and none of the Drive's other files.
